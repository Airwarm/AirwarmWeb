Meet Airwarm

Founded by Thomas Robinson in Baildon, Shipley, Airwarm builds on more than 17 years in plumbing, bathroom installations and tiling through TJR Plumbing & Tiling Solutions, a Which? Trusted Trader.

That experience means careful preparation, tidy work and respect for your home.

Airwarm installations begin in April 2027. Home Energy Assessments, Desktop Reviews, surveys and design are available now.

Qualifications & training

  • Level 3-qualified plumber
  • Low-temperature heating and air source heat pump training
  • NIBE Air Source Installation and Commissioning training, completed 28 October 2026
  • Professional indemnity insurance: £500,000 cover

Airwarm and Ample

Airwarm looks after your home

Your survey, heating design, installation and aftercare are Airwarm’s responsibility. We remain your technical contact.

Ample provides the compliance route

For projects delivered through the partnership, Ample provides the MCS framework and manages the Boiler Upgrade Scheme process for eligible installations and the required DNO application or notification.

Airwarm does not hold its own MCS certification. The certification, grant eligibility and project arrangements are explained in your proposal. Ample is a trading name of Fornax Energy Ltd.
