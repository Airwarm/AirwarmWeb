Why Airwarm is being built this way round

Homeowners are routinely asked to make expensive heating decisions using inconsistent advice. Airwarm exists to reduce that uncertainty: to translate technical requirements into clear choices, design around the property rather than the product, and stay available after the installation is finished.

Built on experience. Focused on heat pumps.

Airwarm is a new specialist heat-pump business, built on more than 17 years of experience working in customers’ homes through TJR Plumbing & Tiling Solutions, a Which? Trusted Trader. Airwarm installations begin in April 2027; Home Energy Assessments, Desktop Engineering Reviews, heat-loss surveys and system design are available now.

The Airwarm approach is straightforward: understand the home first, design the system properly, document the decisions and remain involved after installation.

Why Airwarm exists

The problem it is answering

Heat pumps have a reputation problem in this country, and most of it is deserved by the installations rather than by the technology. A unit sized from a rule of thumb, radiators left as they were, controls on factory defaults and nobody available afterwards will produce a house that is expensive to heat and an owner who tells their neighbours so.

Almost none of that is a heat pump problem. It is a design and aftercare problem, and it is fixable by doing ordinary things carefully and writing them down.

The mission underneath it is narrower and more practical: help homeowners make confident energy decisions by assessing homes honestly, designing appropriate low-temperature heating systems, installing them properly and supporting how they perform over time.

The customer Airwarm is built for

Someone who values correct design, is willing to consider the emitter or fabric work that a calculation actually justifies, understands that the home has to be assessed before anything is promised, and wants an installer who is still accountable after commissioning. If that is you, we will get on well.

Airwarm and Ample

Professional installation. Independent Airwarm design. MCS-backed delivery.

Airwarm works in partnership with Ample, who provide the MCS framework and compliance route behind installations delivered through the partnership. Airwarm is not MCS certified itself, and we will not describe it as though it were.

What does not change is who you deal with. Airwarm remains responsible for your survey, the engineering design, the installation and the aftercare. The partnership adds an additional layer of independent compliance oversight alongside Airwarm’s own engineering and design process.

Who does what

  • Airwarm: survey, design, installation and aftercare
  • Ample partnership: MCS framework, compliance and project infrastructure
  • You: one company to deal with, from the first assessment to the annual service

How the work is delivered

Qualified contractors where they are needed

Electrical work is carried out by appropriately qualified contractors where required, working to Airwarm’s design and coordination. Subcontracting does not move Airwarm’s responsibility to you onto somebody else.

The people who install take part in the survey

Where practical, whoever would do the work is involved in or reviews the survey. It removes the gap between what was promised and what turns out to be possible.

Aftercare as a service, not a favour

48-hour contact, a two-week comfort review, seasonal optimisation where required and an annual service reminder. It protects your outcome and it tells us what to design better next time.

Limits stated out loud

No general public emergency call-out service. No work outside our competence. No properties outside the service area. No projects where a genuinely necessary upgrade is refused.

Frequently asked questions

How will my installation be MCS certified?

Through our partnership with Ample. Ample provide the MCS framework and compliance route behind installations delivered through the partnership, while Airwarm carries out your survey, the engineering design, the installation and the aftercare.

That is not the same as Airwarm holding its own MCS certification, and we are not going to blur the two. The documentation that applies to your project is set out before you commit to anything.

Ample is a trading name of Fornax Energy Ltd. backedbyample.com

Who does the electrical work?

A heat pump is a substantial electrical load, so the incoming supply, main fuse and consumer unit are all checked at survey. Electrical work is carried out by appropriately qualified regional electrical contractors working to Airwarm’s design and coordination, and where the network operator needs to be involved Airwarm will say so early.

Does Airwarm offer emergency call-out?

Not to the general public. Airwarm does not run a 24-hour emergency call-out service, and will not imply otherwise.

Existing Airwarm customers get a response within 24 hours. Where an existing customer has a genuine system fault that cannot be resolved remotely, emergency support or attendance may be provided as appropriate. That is a commitment to respond, not a guarantee that someone will attend within 24 hours. If you are not yet a customer, we normally reply within 2 working days.
