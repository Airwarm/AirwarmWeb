Your NIBE manufacturer warranty

Up to two years’
parts and labour cover.

Choose Airwarm for your NIBE installation and eligible NIBE heat-pump products can receive up to two years’ manufacturer warranty, subject to NIBE’s terms.

We’ll take care of product registration as part of handover, provide the commissioning records and explain the annual servicing needed to maintain cover.

Cover runs for 24 months from installation or 33 months from manufacture, whichever ends sooner. It is NIBE product cover; separate installation and workmanship terms apply.

Read NIBE’s full warranty terms

Keeping your cover in place

  1. Installation & commissioning

    Installed and commissioned in line with NIBE’s requirements, with the Benchmark documents completed.

  2. Registration within 28 days

    Your NIBE products must be registered within 28 days of installation. Keep your confirmation with your handover documents.

  3. Annual servicing & records

    Arrange a service each year and retain the completed service record.

Before you decide

A few useful answers

Does this certificate mean Airwarm is a NIBE Pro installer?

The certificate confirms Thomas’s completion of NIBE’s installation and commissioning course. NIBE Pro is a separate programme. The warranty described here is the standard cover of up to two years.

Does the certificate replace MCS certification?

Manufacturer training and MCS certification serve different purposes. For installations delivered through our Ample partnership, Ample provides the MCS compliance and certification route. Read how the partnership works.

Will you recommend NIBE for every home?

We recommend equipment after assessing the property, heat loss, hot-water requirements and installation constraints. Your survey and design determine whether a NIBE system is suitable.

Who do I contact after installation?

Airwarm remains your first point of contact for your installation and controls. We will explain the manufacturer warranty, servicing requirements and support contacts at handover.
