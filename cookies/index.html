Cookie policy

You can browse this website without accepting anything. Google Analytics is off unless you choose to enable it through Cookie settings in the footer.

Last updated 29 October 2026. Version 2.1.

Your choice

We do not show an automatic cookie pop-up. Use Cookie settings at the bottom of any page to open the optional controls, then choose Accept analytics or Reject analytics. We remember it in your browser for up to six months using local storage under airwarm-analytics-consent-v1. This stores your choice and its date, not your assessment answers. If browser storage is blocked, your choice may not persist and Analytics stays off on the next page.

Optional Google Analytics cookies

If you accept, Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-LJW60F22LD) helps us understand page visits and improve the website. It may set first-party _ga and _ga_LJW60F22LD cookies to distinguish browsers and maintain session information. We configure a cookie lifetime of up to six months; your browser may shorten this.

Before acceptance, and when you reject, we do not load the Google Analytics tag. Advertising consent stays off. We do not send assessment answers or enquiry form contents to Analytics. Our page-view event excludes URL query strings and referrer information.

Changing your mind

Choose Cookie settings, then Reject analytics to stop further analytics collection and remove Analytics cookies accessible to this site. This cannot undo information already sent. You can also delete cookies and site storage through your browser settings.

Other services

The website loads Montserrat from Google Fonts, which receives your browser request and IP address. GitHub Pages hosts the website and processes the requests needed to deliver pages. The Home Energy Assessment keeps your answers in page memory unless you choose to submit an enquiry. See our privacy policy for more details.
