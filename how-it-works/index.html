Your heat-pump journey

Four clear phases, from checking your home to installation and support. You choose whether to proceed after your review and quote.

Check my home

From first check to warm home

  1. Check & review

    Check your home online, then choose whether to send your result to Airwarm for a written Desktop Review.

  2. Survey & design

    We measure your home, design the system and provide a detailed proposal with a fixed quote.

  3. Approval & installation

    We test and set up the system, show you how to use it and support you afterwards.

Questions about the process

What if the assessment says my home is potentially unsuitable?

You will be told which of your answers led to that result. It is a statement about the property’s condition today, not a permanent verdict, and there is nearly always something worth doing even if a heat pump is not the immediate next step.

Do I have to have a survey to get advice?

No. The assessment and our written Desktop Review come first, and both are there to work out whether a survey makes sense at all.

How long does an installation take?

It depends on how much emitter and pipework change is needed, whether a cylinder is being installed where there was none, and what electrical work is required. Airwarm gives you a realistic programme in the proposal rather than a standard figure, and confirms it at the pre-start stage.

Will I be without heating or hot water?

There is normally a period without heating and hot water while the changeover happens. Airwarm plans that period deliberately, tells you how long it is expected to be, and agrees it with you before work starts rather than presenting it as an unavoidable surprise.

What documents will I receive?

A documented design showing the heat loss calculation and its assumptions, the emitter schedule, the equipment specification, a commissioning record, manufacturer documentation and warranty details, and a handover pack explaining how the system is meant to be operated.

What if the survey says my house needs work first?

Then that is what we tell you, along with what specifically would need to change and roughly in what order. Plenty of properties are a good candidate after some fabric or emitter work, and knowing that is a useful answer in itself.
