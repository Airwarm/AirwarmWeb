Accessibility statement

Airwarm wants this website to be usable by as many people as reasonably possible. Accessibility is treated as part of the site design and maintenance, not as a badge or an unsupported claim of certification.

What we do

  • Use semantic headings, labels and landmarks so pages have a logical structure.
  • Provide keyboard-accessible navigation and a skip link to the main content.
  • Use text alternatives for meaningful images and keep decorative images out of the reading order.
  • Use visible focus states and form error messages that are associated with the relevant fields.
  • Avoid relying on colour alone to communicate the Home Energy Assessment result.
  • Design pages to reflow for smaller screens and allow browser text zoom.

Known limitations

Airwarm is a new website and has not been independently certified against a particular accessibility standard. Some third-party content or services linked from the site are outside Airwarm's control. We will update this statement when material accessibility issues are identified or the site changes significantly.

Tell us about a problem

If something on airwarm.co.uk is difficult to use, email hello@airwarm.co.uk or call 01274 947 197. Tell us which page or feature caused the problem and, if useful, what device, browser or assistive technology you were using. We will use that information to investigate and improve the site.

Alternative formats

If you need information from the website in a different accessible format, contact us and explain what you need. We will consider the request and respond with a practical alternative where we reasonably can.
